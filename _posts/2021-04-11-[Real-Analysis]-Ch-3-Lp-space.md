## Inequalities

* Jensen
* Hölder
* Minkowski



## $L^p$ space

### $1\le p<\infty$

* $L^p$ norm, space
* $L^p$ is complete (Banach)
* $L^2$ is Hilbert



### $p=\infty$

* $L^\infty$ norm, space
* $L^\infty$ is complete (Banach)



## Continuous approximation

### $L^p(\mu)$ and $C_c(X)$



* S ~ L^p
* Lusin's theorem
* C_c(X) ~ L^p



### $C_0(X)$ and $C_c(X)$

* C_c is dense in C_0
* C_0 is complete
